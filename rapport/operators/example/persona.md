# Operator — sam

**Fictional example.** This is what the voice interview produces. Yours will be longer and
more specific, because it will have your actual samples and your actual story in it.

Copy this folder, rename it to your slug, and let `npm run onboarding` overwrite it.

## Voice
- Grammar: human, a little loose
- Register: oral, the way I talk
- First message: short, up to 420 characters
- Opens with: Hey Ana,
- Signs off: Best, Sam
- Closes with: a question worth answering
- Language: English

## Never
- synergy
- leverage
- circle back
- touch base
- em dashes
- emoji

## How you actually write

Real samples go here — the mediocre ones too. These are the material the writing is built
from, so paste what you sent, not what you wish you had sent.

> saw you shipped the audit last week. how are you handling the reruns?

> quick one: is the month close still landing on the 5th, or did the new provider push it?

> fair point, most tools do that. what still gets done by hand around it?

## Where the voice comes from

Grew up in Naples, sold restaurant equipment before software, so I talk fast and I ask before
I explain. I lose people when I get technical, so I keep it to one idea per message. I would
rather sound like a person who noticed something than a vendor with a script.
