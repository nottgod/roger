**Fictional example.** The central pain and the vocabulary that makes you sound credible to
each kind of buyer.

## 3.9.M. The central pain (read by gen.mjs)

One line, no `|`. This goes at the top of the diagnosis of every message, which is why it
lives here instead of inside the code.

| key | value |
| --- | --- |
| dor_central | Month close takes a week and nobody fully trusts the number. The finance team is reconciling by hand, so every new currency or payment method makes the problem worse instead of better. |

## 3.11. Gap signature

Two or more of these and the gap is material (see `3.11.M` in `icp.md` for the machine keys):

- quiet in public for months: nobody there is talking about the problem
- messaging that says nothing specific about how they work
- the person who owns the problem never appears anywhere
- the site and the founder tell different stories

## 3.12. Vocabulary and narrative by segment

| Segment | Key narrative (one line) | Vocabulary that sounds like an insider |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **payments** | Every new provider adds a settlement file that nobody owns | settlement file, chargeback, payout window, provider fees |
| **marketplaces** | Split payments mean the ledger never matches the bank on the first try | split payment, seller payout, escrow, take rate |
| **neobanks** | Growth multiplies transaction volume faster than the finance team | ledger entry, suspense account, break, daily close |
| **lending** | Interest accrual and repayments disagree at the end of every month | accrual, amortization, repayment schedule, delinquency |
