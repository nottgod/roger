# Ledgerline — approaches

**Fictional example.** Which approach fits which person. Read at runtime by `gen.mjs`
(table 7.8). The names in the first column are the ones your `icp.md` 3.3.M points at, so the
two files have to agree.

## 7.7. Structure of a first message

- a specific hook: something real you observed about them, never generic
- a consultative observation: a light diagnosis, not a pitch
- one open question, and only one

Under 600 characters, three or four sentences, no formal greeting, no corporate sign-off.

## 7.8. When to use each approach

| Abordagem | Quando usar |
| --- | --- |
| Case | Someone who validates by proof: a head of finance, a controller, anyone who will ask "who else" |
| Contextual | They posted or shipped something recently. Answer that, with substance |
| Direto | A founder who decides alone and reads fast. Say what you do in one line and ask |
| Institucional | A senior finance leader at a regulated company. Sober register, no hype |
| Evento | They will be at an event you will also attend |

## 7.10. What to expect

A specific first touch beats a generic one by a wide margin, and the second touch matters more
than people think: most replies in this pack came from touch two or three, not touch one.
