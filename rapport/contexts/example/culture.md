# Ledgerline — culture

**Fictional example.** How people expect to be addressed, by region. Read at runtime by
`gen.mjs` (table 8.1): the region names in the first column are the ones the engine maps
geographies to, so keep them as they are and change the other two columns.

## 8.1. Register by region

| Região | Registro | Abertura exemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USA NY | direct, bottom line first, no warm-up | saw you shipped X last week |
| UK | dry, understated, no superlatives | noticed X, curious how you handle Y |
| Germânico (DE/CH) | precise and factual, no empty praise, no hype | your docs say X, how does that work with Y |
| Latin | warmer, a line of relationship before the point | tudo bem? vi que vocês lançaram X |
| East Asia (JP/KR) | formal, patient, indirect ask | apologies for the direct message, a question about X |

## Transversal

- Germanic: never open with a compliment. State the observation.
- East Asia: one ask, and give room. A follow-up too fast reads as pressure.
- Anywhere: the opening line has to prove you looked. A generic opener is worse than nothing.
